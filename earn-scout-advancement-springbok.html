Your award at a glance

Our original award overview helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Adventure

    Lead navigation for two or more Patrols with ten bearings. Lead a new-route expedition: three days, two nights, at least 30 km on foot and four Scouts including you; other participants at First Class or below. Submit the log within four weeks.

  2. Camp and serve

    Lead a two-night camp for at least two Patrols, without leading either Patrol. Undertake at least forty active service hours on a Court-of-Honour-ratified project. Expedition, camp, service and major-project plans require advance regional approval.

  3. Lead and specialise

    Complete a listed safety badge and emergency-services activity, public Scouting presentation, two staged first-aid incidents, a major-project option and the leadership requirements, including six Court of Honour meetings and an office for a term. This is an overview; use the full standard for all conditions and options.
