Your award at a glance

Our original award overview helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Assess your Patrol

    Use the fifteen disciplines covering membership, roles, attendance, advancement, training, councils, activities, hikes, camps, service and the National Challenge. Keep the required evidence in your private Patrol records.

  2. Check your level

    Participation covers one to four disciplines, Bronze five to eight, Silver nine to twelve and Gold thirteen to fifteen. Use the full current scoring standard; service is measured in Scout-hours, not simply elapsed event time.
