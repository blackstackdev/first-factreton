Recruiter Gold

Cubs and Scouts can earn Gold by recruiting five new members across Meerkats, Cubs or Scouts.

Your award at a glance

Our original award overview helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Support your recruits

    All five must be invested before the badge can be awarded.

  2. Check wearing dates

    Confirm renewal and wearing dates with your leader; the standard normally permits wearing until the end of the following calendar year unless another member is recruited.
