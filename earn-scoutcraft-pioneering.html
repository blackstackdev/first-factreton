Pioneering

Recommended age: 12–14, rather than a compulsory minimum. Arrange equipment, supervision and approval with your examiner.

Learn · Practise · Show

What you’ll work on

Our original requirements summary helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Learn the foundations

    Whip both rope ends. Demonstrate bowline, clove hitch, figure-eight, fisherman’s, marlinspike, reef, rolling hitch, round turn and two half hitches, sheepshank, sheet bend, taut-line and timber hitch, with their uses. Demonstrate diagonal, figure-of-eight, round, sheer and square lashings.

  2. Understand safe pioneering

    Discuss SSA’s pioneering practice note, including planning, equipment care and safe use.

  3. Choose a project route

    Either complete two approved small projects, alone or with a Patrol of four to six; OR complete one small project and participate in a larger team project. The full standard lists the eligible builds and dimensions. Agree your choices before construction; this guide does not approve a structure to carry people.
