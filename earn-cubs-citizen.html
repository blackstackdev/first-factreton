What you’ll work on

Our original requirements summary helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Know your community

    Research the current President, Deputy President and provincial Premier, and include capital-city pictures in your notebook. Explain two local historic places and why laws matter.

  2. Choose ways to participate

    Options include a national role model, local helping organisations, a community good turn, five good citizens, learning about another local ethnic group, or government services. The full standard defines each choice. Check officeholders at the time of assessment.
