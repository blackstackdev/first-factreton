Complete Aviation Skills Scoutcraft and re-demonstrate it OR complete all its requirements. Recommended age: 14+.

What you’ll work on

Our original requirements summary helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Observe aircraft

    Learn safe, lawful spotting and record thirty aircraft across at least five sessions at two or more locations.

  2. Research aviation

    Investigate registration prefixes from twenty specified countries, ten international airlines serving South Africa and ten air-force insignia, meeting the required African representation.

  3. Choose an exploration

    Visit an aviation location OR design an original aircraft. Share the prescribed observations or design details with your examiner.
