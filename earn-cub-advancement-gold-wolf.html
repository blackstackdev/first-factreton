Your award at a glance

Our original award overview helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Develop four challenges

    Continue Awareness, Community, Outdoor and Aptitude, with Promise and Law. The full programme includes nature, service, practical skills, safety and helping younger Cubs.

  2. Go outdoors

    Among the requirements are two camping nights, including a night in a tent or shelter you helped pitch, and a trail using twenty woodcraft signs. These are examples within a larger programme.

  3. Choose your interests

    Earn four Interest badges, at least one per challenge; any four subjects may be chosen. Work through all detailed requirements with your Pack Scouter.

Your next step

Give it a go.

Choose a goal with your leader, arrange the right supervision and practise. Agree how you will demonstrate your own skills and keep any evidence privately.
