Swimmer

Complete Swimming Scoutcraft and re-demonstrate it OR complete its requirements. Recommended age: 14–17.

Explore the steps ↓

Learn · Practise · Show

What you’ll work on

Our original requirements summary helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Develop technique and stamina

    Demonstrate the five specified 50 m stroke tasks, a continuous 250 m swim, controlled entries and water-hazard understanding.

  2. Practise and lead

    Complete the supervised rescue and survival demonstrations, then choose an adult-supervised teaching or activity-leadership option. Check exact distances, floating times and permitted stroke adaptations with the examiner.

Your next step

Make a plan.
Give it a go.

Choose a goal with your leader, arrange the right supervision and practise. Agree how you will demonstrate your own skills and keep any evidence privately.

Reading this guide or attending an activity does not award a badge or issue a licence. Your leader arranges the appropriate assessment.
