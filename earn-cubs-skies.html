What you’ll work on

Our original requirements summary helps you plan. It is not the complete SSA assessment checklist. Confirm every task, choice and current requirement with your leader before assessment.

  1. Explore the sky

    Options are modelling the Solar System, explaining stars versus planets, identifying three southern constellations, or making pinhole planetariums of three constellations.

  2. Observe and explain

    Other options cover safe solar observation and Earth’s orbit, or observing and describing the Moon. Agree equipment, supervision and chosen tasks with your examiner.
